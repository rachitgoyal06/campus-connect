About MUJ Campus Connect

MUJ Campus Connect is a student-exclusive marketplace created for students of Manipal University Jaipur. The platform enables students to buy, sell and rent products within the campus community, making transactions safer, easier and more affordable.

Why Use MUJ Campus Connect?

🛒

Buy Products

Find books, electronics, hostel essentials and much more from fellow students.

💰

Sell Products

List products you no longer need and earn money quickly.

📦

Rent Products

Rent calculators, lab equipment, hostel essentials and more.
